Cookie Notice

Last updated: October 2026

This website sets no cookies of its own for advertising. It uses one optional analytics tool, which only runs if you say yes, and a few services that load from other companies.

Analytics (optional)

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, I use Google Analytics to see how people use the site, such as which pages are read and on what kind of device. It sets cookies called _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID, which last up to two years. If you click Decline, or ignore the banner, Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

Search Console

I use Google Search Console to see how the site appears in Google search. It works on Google's side and sets no cookies on your device.

Fonts

The site loads its typeface (Poppins) from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser contacts Google, which receives your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies for this.

Hosting

The site is delivered by Cloudflare, which may use strictly necessary cookies for security and performance.

Waitlist

The waitlist form on the Join the waitlist page is provided by Kit. Loading it runs Kit's script on that page, and the form includes a security check (reCAPTCHA) from Google. Kit and Google may set their own cookies or similar technologies there, under their own policies. If you prefer not to use the form, you can email me at hello@paulwelshcoaching.com.

Changing your mind

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time.
