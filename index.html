What coaching actually includes

  • A plan built around where you are

    Strength fundamentals, beginner calisthenics and mobility, sequenced so the first few weeks are genuinely achievable. Not a programme borrowed from someone who has trained for a decade.

  • Technique you can trust

    Every movement is taught step by step, with form feedback on your own videos, so you are never guessing whether you are doing it right.

  • A clear way to progress

    Every exercise has an easier and a harder version, so you always know your next step and when you are ready for it.

  • Real 1:1 coaching, remotely

    Regular video check-ins with someone who knows your plan, wherever you train.

  • Food that fits real life

    Practical, no-fuss nutrition guidance. Not a meal plan you will abandon by Thursday.

  • Someone who answers

    Direct messaging access between check-ins. Coaching, not just a programme PDF.
