# Paul Welsh Coaching: product truth

**What it is.** Online coaching in strength, beginner calisthenics, movement and nutrition guidance (movement quality is a by-product of training, not a headline offer). Coach: Paul (Level 2 and 3 PT, 10+ years training, based in Spain), fully remote, clients anywhere.

**Audience.** Adults of any age starting out: new to training, starting again, or training but lacking structure. Wary of being sold to. Targeted at beginners without saying "only beginners". Paul has no clients yet; the site launches his PT career, so do not imply client history.

**Job of the site (Persuade).** Make a nervous beginner feel "this is for me", then take the next step. Launch phase (until coaching spots open): the next step is joining the waitlist at /book-a-call/ (native form into Kit, no embed). Once Paul is taking clients, switch the page back to booking a free 15 minute call (BOOKING_URL in tools/build.mjs).

**Brand.** Dark, calm, athletic. Poppins. Palette: Black #0d1419, Blue Black #192530, Ice Blue #c6d5e1, Off White #eff1f3. Arc-line motif, P dumbbell mark, photography of dark moody studio training. Taglines: Show up. Start small. Build strength. / Your first rep starts here. / Movement for real life. / Build the basics.

**Voice.** Direct, warm, never gym-bro. No fabricated credentials, testimonials, statistics or prices.

**Constraints.** Multi-page static HTML/CSS/JS. SEO and AEO structure from the copy brief (direct answers first, FAQPage schema, dated content, crawler-friendly robots.txt). Waitlist form posts to Kit (KIT_FORM_URL); Google Calendar appointment page is kept for when booking reopens.
